Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about KryptLedg.

What exchanges does KryptLedg support?

KryptLedg currently supports Binance and Coinbase CSV reports. We plan to add more exchanges based on demand.

Are my files stored?

Your files are processed in memory and are not persistently stored on our servers after processing is complete.

What reports can I generate?

KryptLedg offers three report packages: Free Report ($0, up to 100 transactions), Standard Report ($9, up to 5,000 transactions with full P&L), and Complete Report ($19, unlimited transactions with full reconciliation and tax-ready export).

How does KryptLedg calculate gains?

KryptLedg uses FIFO (First In, First Out) cost basis calculation. Each disposal is matched against the earliest available acquisition lot for the same asset, producing an auditable realized gain or loss.

Can KryptLedg detect transfers?

Yes. KryptLedg can match deposit and withdrawal pairs that share the same asset, quantity, and timestamp to identify internal wallet transfers and exclude them from taxable events.

Can I export my report?

Yes. After processing, you can export your transaction analysis and accounting results as CSV files for use in your tax software or with your accountant.

Is KryptLedg a tax filing service?

No. KryptLedg is a reporting and analysis tool. It helps you organize transaction data, calculate gains, and produce reports. It does not file tax returns on your behalf.
